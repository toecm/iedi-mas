# Engineering novelty boundary

## Defensible research idea

The research idea is **counterfactual-context-gated selective
interpretation**: before committing to a candidate meaning, a runtime wrapper
tests whether the scorer is invariant to reviewed meaning-preserving context
changes and moves toward a specified contrasting sense under reviewed minimal
meaning-changing changes. A calibrated policy converts those signals into
commitment, one-slot clarification, or abstention.

The distinctive object is the integrated gate for ambiguous expressions drawn
from three bounded English-use community resources together with its typed
context policy and action-labelled, family-held-out evaluation. It is not any
one of its components, and it does not claim global World-English coverage.

## Established or inherited ingredients

- IUUY: multi-agent interaction roles, clarification, feedback, ASR and
  provenance directions.
- IEDI: IEDID, the three-variety scope, fuzzy lookup and proposed LLM fallback.
- KICS-W: persona/context injection, tone/situation metadata and dynamic model
  routing.
- External literature: behavioral invariance/directional tests, contrastive
  context changes, selective classification, calibration/conformal control,
  structured uncertainty, information-value clarification and model routing.

These must be disclosed as foundations or baselines.

## Potentially protectable implementation details to document, not claim

If an institutional technology-transfer office considers an invention
disclosure, document the exact implemented combination, including:

- the schema and prohibition policy for ephemeral context cards;
- generation and validation of paired preserving/changing operator bundles;
- the joint verification feature computation;
- hard conflict/provenance overrides and calibrated action policy;
- one-question slot utility and re-verification sequence; and
- gate-triggered, benefit-constrained model escalation with complete logs.

counsel/technology-transfer review are required. Publication can affect filing
rights in some jurisdictions, so coordinate before public disclosure if a
filing is contemplated. Do not write “patented,” “patent pending,” “novel,” or
“first” in the manuscript without documentary support.
